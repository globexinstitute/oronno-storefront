Shipping & Returns Policy

1. Nationwide Shipping Rates

We ship across all 64 districts of Bangladesh via premier courier networks (Steadfast, Pathao, Paperfly):

  • Inside Dhaka: Delivery fee of ৳80. Typical transit time: 2 to 3 business days.
  • Outside Dhaka: Delivery fee of ৳150. Typical transit time: 3 to 5 business days.

2. Cash on Delivery (COD) Guidelines

Customers choosing Cash on Delivery are requested to inspect the external packaging in front of the courier delivery person.

3. 7-Day Exchange Policy

If an item does not fit or contains a manufacturing defect, you can exchange it within 7 days of delivery under the following conditions:

  • The dress must be unworn, unwashed, and in its original boutique packaging with tags intact.
  • Custom-tailored dresses are non-returnable unless a tailoring error was made on our end.

4. Assistance & Support

To initiate an exchange or inquire about a delayed parcel, please contact our support hotline or WhatsApp directly at 01881896612.
