Our Identity

The Meaning of Oronno

In Bengali, Oronno translates to "forest"—a symbol of deep roots, organic beauty, natural balance, and quiet majesty. Our brand was established in Dhaka to bring women an authentic, carefully curated destination for Pakistani suits and ethnic wear that never compromises on fabric purity or stitching perfection.

"Not just fashion, it's a feeling — wear grace, own the moment."

We curate three distinct tiers of dresses: everyday breathable cotton lawns for polished casual settings, resplendent organzas for family festivals, and opulent micro-chiffons with laser cutwork for celebrations.

Our Quality Promise

Carefully Selected, Quality Assured

Every single piece that arrives at our studio in Donia, Dhaka is closely inspected. We examine thread tension, neck embroidery symmetry, dupatta scalloping, and trouser stitching before an outfit is packaged.

Because standard off-the-rack sizing does not always suit every silhouette, we offer a dedicated custom sizing service. Our tailoring team accommodates your exact bust, waist, kameez length, and armhole preferences.
